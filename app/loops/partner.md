# Partner Prospector Loop — 44i Digital (Billy)

Produce scored, enriched, send-ready prospective **whitelabel partners** for the 44i Digital
program. These are RESELLER partners: they sell digital under their own brand, 44i fulfills.
They are NOT end clients.

## Four qualifying verticals

| Vertical | Sweet spot | Decision-makers |
|---|---|---|
| TV/Radio Broadcasters | Independent/family-owned, single station to multi-market clusters, local-direct-heavy | GM, Market Manager, Owner, VP Sales, DOS |
| Advertising Agencies | Full-service traditional/creative shops with digital gaps (no in-house SEM/programmatic/OTT) | Owner, Principal, President, Partner |
| Publishers | Local/regional newspapers, magazines, community publications with legacy sales teams | Publisher, GM, Ad Director |
| OOH Groups | Independent billboard/out-of-home operators | Owner, GM, VP Sales |

## Three buyer doors and their detectable signals

| Door | Signals |
|---|---|
| **Fresh Start** (never had white-label) | No digital services listed anywhere; dated site; purely traditional ad offerings |
| **Frustrated Refugee** (burned by a provider) | Thin/templated digital services page; white-label competitor tech footprints; vendor-churn hints |
| **Gap Filler** (partial in-house digital) | Lists social/web but NOT programmatic/OTT/SEM/geofencing; "we partner with vendors" language; digital-seller job postings |

## Hard disqualifiers — apply before scoring

- Owned by a mega-group with in-house digital (Nexstar, Gray, Sinclair, Scripps, Tegna, iHeart,
  Audacy, Cumulus, Townsquare, Saga, Hubbard, Gannett, Lee, Hearst, McClatchy — verify current
  status if uncertain)
- Digital-first agencies (fulfillment is their core business — they are competitors)
- Existing 44i / 44i Digital partners, or any company on the exclusion list supplied in the task
- Non-US companies

## Fit score (0–100)

+25 vertical match · +20 independent ownership · +20 detectable door signal ·
+15 decision-maker named · +10 direct email/phone found · +10 metro population 50k–1M

## Sourcing

FCC facility data for broadcast (filter by state, exclude mega-group owners), state broadcaster
and press association member rosters, AAF chapter and agency directories, OAAA for OOH, and web
search for "[vertical] [metro]". Verify ownership independence with a search whenever it is not
obvious from the company's own site.

## Door-matched opening hooks (language from 44idigital.com)

- **Fresh Start** → "we make this simple," revenue guarantee, no risk
- **Frustrated Refugee** → "a partner that actually picks up the phone," month-to-month,
  100% US-based Sioux Falls fulfillment
- **Gap Filler** → "we slot in around the team you already have — if you have a social person,
  we don't touch social"

Keep hooks in plain English. Billy reads these on his phone between calls.
