# Client Prospector Loop — 44i (Darren)

Produce scored, enriched, send-ready prospective **direct clients** for 44i (44i.com). These are
end clients where 44i becomes agency of record — full service: video production, website, SEO,
social, targeted display, OTT/CTV, digital billboards, reputation management, branding, radio,
broadcast TV. This is NOT the whitelabel reseller program (that is Billy's partner loop).

## Five qualifying verticals

| Vertical | Sweet spot | Decision-makers |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | Independent/regional hospitals, critical-access hospitals, multi-location clinics, specialty groups, senior living | Marketing Director, Director of Marketing & Communications, CMO, CEO (small orgs) |
| Casinos | Tribal and commercial casinos/resorts, especially expanding or newly opened properties | Marketing Director, Director of Player Development, GM |
| Higher Ed | Private colleges, technical/community colleges, small regional universities fighting the enrollment cliff | VP Marketing & Communications, Director of Admissions/Enrollment Marketing, VP Enrollment |
| Ag | Seed companies, equipment dealers/manufacturers, ag-tech, co-ops, livestock genetics — regional to national reach | Marketing Manager/Director, VP Sales & Marketing, Owner |
| Retail | Multi-location regional retailers (furniture, appliance, farm & ranch, auto adjacent, grocery) | Owner, Marketing Director, VP Marketing |

Target the person with budget authority to hire an agency: Marketing Director / CMO / VP Marketing
first, Owner / CEO / GM for smaller organizations. Skip coordinators and specialists.

## Three buyer doors and their detectable signals

| Door | Signals |
|---|---|
| **No Agency** (in-house/ad hoc marketing) | Dated site, no video content, sporadic social posting, no OTT/display footprint, generic stock branding |
| **Agency Switcher** (has an agency, restless) | Public agency-of-record RFPs (common in higher ed, healthcare, tribal gaming), new CMO/marketing director hire, marketing job postings signaling churn, visibly stale campaigns |
| **Growth Moment** (money in motion) | Expansion/new location/new facility announcements, casino expansions or new gaming compacts, rebrands, mergers, new service lines, enrollment initiatives, anniversary campaigns |

## Hard disqualifiers — apply before scoring

- Existing 44i clients, or any organization on the exclusion list supplied in the task
- **Competitive conflict**: same vertical AND same market as a current 44i client. Flag rather than
  silently drop when uncertain — Scott decides.
- Mega-systems with large in-house agencies or locked national AOR relationships (major health
  systems, flagship state universities, national casino corporations such as Caesars/MGM/Boyd-owned
  properties)
- Organizations too small to sustain agency-of-record spend (single-doc practices, single-location
  boutiques, sub-1,000-student schools with no marketing staff)
- Outside the geography supplied in the task

## Fit score (0–100)

+25 vertical match · +20 budget capacity (org size in sweet spot, evidence of media spend) ·
+20 detectable door signal · +15 decision-maker named · +10 direct email/phone found ·
+10 growth-moment recency (signal less than 6 months old)

## Sourcing

State hospital association rosters, tribal gaming commission and state gaming board lists, state
private-college and technical-college directories, ag manufacturer and seed association member
lists, and web search for "[vertical] [metro]". Watch RFP boards (state procurement, tribal
notices, higher-ed RFP listings) for Agency Switcher signals. A 30-second video/social audit — is
there video? when was the last post? — separates No Agency from Agency Switcher.

## Contact enrichment — source hierarchy and confidence

1. **The organization's own website** — staff directories, leadership pages, press releases,
   contact pages → `verified`
2. **Regulatory / nonprofit filings** — GuideStar/Candid profiles and 990s name officers, often
   with direct phones (hospitals, colleges, senior living) → `verified`
3. **News coverage** — local business journals and trade press quote marketing directors by name
   and title → `verified` for the name; check the article date, titles go stale
4. **Third-party databases** — use for triangulation only. If two or more independent sources
   agree on a name/title/email → `high`. Never treat a single paywalled snippet as fact.
5. **Email-pattern inference** — when the domain and the organization's format are known, an
   inferred address is `pattern`. Always labeled, never presented as verified.

Hard rules:

- **Never fabricate an email address.** If the handle cannot be found, give the main line and the
  route ("call and ask for marketing") instead.
- `pattern` addresses must be verified before any bulk send — bounces damage the sending domain.
- If the only source naming a person is more than two years old, say the name needs a LinkedIn pass
  rather than presenting it as current.
- Fallback ladder: named marketing lead → CEO/Owner/GM (small orgs) → main line with a named ask →
  LinkedIn DM.

Also capture, when you see it: **warm paths** (a contact who previously worked at another target or
a current 44i client) and **hiring signals** (an open marketing role means the seat is empty or
churning — both a door signal and a timing pitch).
